# Code of conduct

Be respectful. This space is for discussing the work in this repository.

**Expected:** courteous, specific, constructive feedback; patience with questions; assuming good intent.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing someone else's private information, spam, or posting content you do not have the right to share.

Issues and comments that break these rules may be edited, hidden or deleted, and repeat offenders may be blocked from the repository. To report a conduct problem, email tim_jones175@icloud.com.
